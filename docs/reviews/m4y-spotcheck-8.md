## Check 3: tick order, timing and sampling

**No test in either world depends on tick order, delegate order or wall-clock time, and none can pass or fail by luck.**
- World 5: the director, pool, beetle, hatch, banner and keeper have `bCanEverTick = false` and `ADojoTestActor` does not tick; everything runs on `FTimerManager`. `FDojoTestWorld::Tick` adds 1/60 s per frame. A timer set before the first tick sits in the pending set and fires one frame late (frame 61, then 121, 181), while later timers fall on exact multiples of 60 (`TimerManager.cpp:1378-1388`). The samples sit 5 frames after (66, 126, 186), rests 12 frames after (258 against 246) and the banner's rest 9 frames after (160 against 151). The boss `PlayNight` swats every 15 frames, so timers coincide with chunk ends, but its results (23 swats, Done, `Created <= 3`, finish above 20 s) do not depend on which side a timer lands.
- World 7: only the gate ticks. Plate, lamp, crate, runner, pickup, door, chime and the reference director do not. Every state change is synchronous (`SetActorLocation`, overlap, `Recount`, `Broadcast`). `UWorld::Tick` runs PrePhysics (`LevelTick.cpp:1750`), the timer manager (`:1816`), then PostUpdateWork (`:1877`); the gate ticks before the reset timer, and the assertions after a reset see at least one sink step either way. There is one listener per event in the boss tests, and the two lamps on one plate (`03:448-449`) are independent, so dynamic multicast order (`ScriptDelegates.h` removes with `RemoveAtSwap`) never matters.
- Margins in World 7 are small but not coincident: the gate open check samples frame 49 against arrival at frame 48 (`05:298-304`, step `375 x (1/60f) = 6.2500005`, so 48 steps reach 300), 25 against 24 (`:417-421`), the boss reset at frame 122 against 120, the gate open at 50 against 48, and the 0.5 s knob at 35 against 30. One frame of lateness cannot flip a result, but two of the margins are two frames (D15 in World 5 is the real tie, below).
- **One real sampling tie, in World 5** (D15, Should): the banner's rest line shows rest seconds rounded up (`05:118, 121, 201`), but the values tested are 2.5, 3.0 and 2.5. `FMath::RoundToInt(2.5f)` is 3, so rounding to nearest passes and only floor is caught. Same tie as spot-check 7's A14. Also broadcast `OnRestStarted(2, 2.2f)` and expect `3 s`.
- The mutations (5 in World 5, 11 in World 7) are deterministic and not order- or timing-sensitive. Untested but derivable: World 5 a hard-coded interval of 1.0, `EndPlay` not stopping timers, round-instead-of-ceil, nearest and tie rules; World 7 a polled board, a Tick-based reset, an uncleared timer, a duplicate bind (K12).

## Check 4: the clamp metadata move (A1541)

`clamp-min-meta` takes the aliases ClampMax, UIMin and UIMax (its term is ClampMin), moved from `meta-specifier`. Findings:

- **The glossary is clean.** No duplicate alias or term across the 269 entries (checked with a read-only script). `meta-specifier.long` keeps the example `meta=(ClampMin = "0", ClampMax = "100")`, which is text and not an alias, so there is no conflict. Term order is fine: `m7-tuning-knobs` is a concept quest that teaches `clamp-min-meta` itself, `m7-plate-gate` lists it after, `meta-specifier` is taught earlier in `f1-uproperty-specifiers`, and the verifier shows no term-order warning.
- **The card is true in 5.8.** ClampMin and ClampMax clamp a typed value in Details (`PropertyHandleImpl.cpp:1043-1062`, `NumericPropertyParams.h:78-79`); UIMin and UIMax set only the slider range (`NumericPropertyParams.h:75-80`); `Units = "s"` and `"cm"` are valid (`UnitConversion.cpp:34, 111`); code, tests and the Blueprint set the value unclamped; a float divided by zero gives infinity with no crash, and `FInterpConstantTo` then returns the target (`UnrealMathUtility.h:1503-1504`).
- **What the move changes for a Pack 1 learner.** The editor's hover (`glossaryProviders.ts:30-45`) matches every alias with no filter on the quest's own terms or on progress (only completions are filtered by `questTermIds`). 79 quest files outside World 7 use ClampMin or ClampMax (11 in Pack 1, 43 in Pack 2, 17 in Pack 3, 8 in Pack 4), nearly all as code. In every one of them, hovering `ClampMin`, `ClampMax`, `UIMin` or `UIMax` now opens the `clamp-min-meta` card, whose `firstQuestId` is `m7-tuning-knobs`, a Pack 3 quest. The text of the card is self-contained and accurate, so the learner gets a correct card; nothing is hidden or broken. But `f1-uproperty-specifiers` (prose `:46`, code `:64`, question `:86-91`) and `10-build-practice-bell` list `meta-specifier` while no spelling in them opens that card any more (K14).
- **The Pack 1 quest that teaches it says something the new card contradicts.** `03-uproperty-specifiers.md:91` explains `fill-clamp`: "ClampMin sets the lowest value the Details panel and Blueprint pins accept." The new card says the clamps guard the editor only. I found no ClampMin handling for graph pins in `Editor/GraphEditor` or `Editor/Kismet` (only property-editor numeric entry honours it), so the "Blueprint pins" half looks wrong. I could not prove it by running the editor (K15).
- Q01 and the boss use the ClampMin spelling in prose without listing `clamp-min-meta` (Note).
