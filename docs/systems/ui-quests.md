# System: ui-quests

Owner: builder-ui-quests. Folder: `src/renderer/src/screens/quests/` and `components/quests/`. Research: `research/gamified-learning-platforms.md` sections 3 and 5 (session shape).

## Screens

Concept: Predict question first (guess, no penalty), then the card (TermText), an editable example in a Monaco scratch buffer with a syntax-check button, then 3 to 5 questions (multiple choice, fill blank, which pattern, spot the bug, explain in one sentence with the model answer revealed after grading). Terms enter the scheduler on completion.

Drill: 6 to 10 items mixed; item renderers for recall, pattern discrimination, Parsons reorder (drag lines into order, distractors marked after), explain in one sentence (AI graded, free). Shows "due for review" on misses, never "lost". Ends with the next due date and keeps the streak.

Breakdown: spec on the left (TermText, acceptance criteria); React Flow board in the center with piece cards (kind: class, component, data, event, step; a purpose label required on each; edges for "owns", "listens to", "spawns", "then"); no hints during the attempt; Submit shows the learner's plan beside one reference plan, asks for at least one revision note, then grades; the result shows required pieces covered, concerns, strengths, and the alternative note. Pieces exposed as unknown are pushed to the next drill.

Build and refactor: three-column layout. Left: brief with hover terms, concepts, pattern chips, rubric, "See it in the editor" checklist, scaffold level badge. Center: Monaco with file tabs, hover provider (glossary), completion (quest terms), autosave every few seconds and on blur, worked-level marked lines with the self-explanation gate. Right: Run (free; never counts against the spree), Submit, Open Editor, build progress (loop mode, phase, elapsed, last log line, cancel), tests list, filtered build log, hint spells (remaining and XP cost), Seer Stone offer after two failed submits, Parsons fallback offer after two failed submits. Keyboard: Cmd/Ctrl+Enter Run, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Enter Submit, Cmd/Ctrl+S save.

Loot: animated score, XP itemized (base, difficulty multiplier, first-pass, spree bonus, hint costs), level and role changes, card unlocks and tier upgrades flipping in, skill changes up and down, spree, streak, chest if any, next due dates; then the review: summary, rubric lines met or not, findings grouped by severity with "why?" (free), reflection question with a one-sentence answer box; then the alternative with its tradeoff note; buttons: Improve, See it run, Next.

Boss: breakdown first, then a piece picker (the learner chooses the order), each piece opens as a build quest, then the integration build with the integration rubric.

## Done criteria

- Playwright flow with the sample content through concept, drill, breakdown, build (fail with a hint, then pass), loot, next quest.
- The editor stays responsive during a build (all process work is in main).
- Every reward moment has one motion; nothing else animates.

Choice order: multiple-choice and which-pattern choices are shuffled each time an item is shown; the stored `answerIndex` refers to the authored order (A61).

Pattern chips: a quest's `patterns[]` are not shown before the quest is passed (or, in a breakdown, before the reference plan is revealed), so a pattern is never named before it is built (A64).

## Architecture (keep current)

Last updated by M5.1h (the Build screen explains failures that are not the learner's code), after M2.2b (flow bugs from the real-engine run).

### Routes and hand-off
- Three routes outside the rail, all in `app/router/routes.ts`, with their values in the hash query (A350): `#/quest?id=<quest id>` (`screens/QuestScreen.tsx` loads the quest and shows the screen for its kind, or the locked reason), `#/drill?kind=due|test-out|parsons-fallback&world=&quest=` (`screens/DrillScreen.tsx`), `#/loot?quest=<quest id>` (`screens/LootScreen.tsx`). `hashForRoute(id, parameters)`, `routeParametersFromHash`, and `useRouteParameters` carry the query. The rail keeps World Map lit on quest and loot, Studio on drills.
- Entry points: World Map stages open their quest on click (locked ones do not), Studio "Start drill" opens a due drill, a contract's "Test out" opens that world's test-out drill, Studio "Continue" opens the quest.
- A graded attempt goes to the loot screen through `screens/quests/loot/useLootStore.ts` (Zustand): the quest, the `AttemptOutcome`, and what the grader said (concept results, breakdown grade, or the run, review, and submitted files). Reloading the loot route without an entry says so and points back.

### Data
- Everything goes through the M2.1 data layer. New pieces: `data/useQuestRun.ts` (a Run or Submit with its run id, progress events, answer, cancel), `data/useDraftAutosave.ts` (`saveQuestDraft` 3 seconds after the last change and at once on blur, window blur, Cmd/Ctrl+S, and leaving), `questKey(id)` and `glossaryAliases` in `data/queryKeys.ts`. Actions take their inputs as arguments, never from a closure, because `useDojoAction` keeps its first action.
- Rule numbers (hint cost share, failed submits before offers, pass score, card tiers) come from `getRules` only.
- The fake (A351): scenario `quests` (`VITE_DOJO_FAKE=quests`) is the returning learner with the map's first world (which mirrors the real Pack 1 World 1, A390) reset to not started, in `data/fake/fixtures/questFlowFixture.ts`: every stage open but the boss, and World 1's quests, attempts, rematches, and card use cases removed from the save. The real World 1 comes from `virtual:dojo-fake-world`, which `tools/fake/fakeWorldPlugin.ts` builds from `content/` whenever the app or the unit tests are built (no committed copy, A365); an empty world is served, with a build warning, if World 1 cannot be built. Invented map stages open a real quest of the same kind under their own id and title. `data/fake/FakeQuestFlow.ts` holds the quest half of the contract: `FakeItemGrader` (A314, A315 rules), `FakeBuildJudge` (brackets compile; the training dummy is checked for real; other quests pass once every file changed), `QuestRunSimulator` (progress events), `FakeReviews` (explain, code, and breakdown graders), `FakeQuestLedger` (XP, spree, streak, cards, skills, unlocks), `FakeDrillSessions`. With Claude Code chosen in Settings, the fake's first review times out into tests-only with Retry review (A363).

### Shared quest components (`components/quests/`, plus `components/TermText.tsx` and `components/TermHoverCard.tsx`)
- `TermText`: quest Markdown (react-markdown, GFM) with glossary hover cards, linked by content-engine's TermLinker (`src/shared/content/termLinker.ts`) whose `glossary-term` element maps to `TermHoverCard` (A352). `lib/matchGlossaryTerm.ts` only serves the editor's hover. `TermHoverCard`: short definition, Read more (Codex), Drill me; opening it records a hover. The card opens on a real pointer move onto the term (then after a 250 ms delay) or on keyboard focus, once per pointer entry (Escape keeps it closed until the pointer leaves and enters again), never when content appears under a pointer that has not moved, such as the predict reveal landing under the "Lock in my guess" click, so it cannot cover the continue button (W9, A1080). `hooks/useIntentionalHover.ts` holds that rule over `PointerTrail` in `lib/pointerMovement.ts`, which the Codex search palette's row highlight also uses.
- `CodeEditor`: Monaco, lazily loaded. `monaco/monacoSetup.ts` imports the editor API, the features used (hover, suggest, find, folding, comments, brackets, multicursor, and a few more), C++, the codicons, one bundled worker, and a theme read from the tokens; no CDN (A353). `monaco/glossaryProviders.ts` registers glossary hover (identifiers) and completion (the quest's terms). `monaco/MonacoCodeEditor.tsx`: one model per quest file, marked-line decorations, word wrap, Cmd/Ctrl+Enter, Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Enter, Cmd/Ctrl+S.
- `QuestionInput` (every question type; multiple-choice and which-pattern choices shuffled per presentation by `lib/presentationOrder.ts`, answers by authored index, A61), `ChoiceList` (native radios, arrow keys), `SpotTheBugInput`, `QuestionFeedback` (a miss reads "due for review"), `ParsonsInput` (A314 line ids; click, drag, or Alt+Up/Down and Delete; after a check decoys are marked, and real lines outside the longest in-order run, from `parsonsPlacement.ts`), `CodeBlock`, `QuestTitleRow` (never wraps: the title truncates, so the save status cannot change the row height and move the buttons below it mid-click, A1264), `QuestStepRail`, `PatternChips` (shown only after a pass or the reference reveal, A64), `AutosaveIndicator`.

### Screens
- Concept (`screens/quests/concept/`): Predict (guess, lock in, the right answer and why), Card (card text, concepts, the example in Monaco with Check syntax and Reset), Questions (all answered, graded once by `submitConcept`, feedback in place with the explain model answer), then the loot.
- Drill (`DrillScreen.tsx`, `screens/quests/drill/`): starts once, one item at a time, Check then Next (Cmd/Ctrl+Enter for both), the next due time under each answer, then the finish card (right count, XP, streak, next due date, chest, test-out; `drillSummaryCopy.ts` counts graded items only, names self-checked explain items on their own line, and shows "N explained" instead of "0/0" when nothing was graded, A940, A941). The Parsons fallback returns to its quest.
- Breakdown (`screens/quests/breakdown/`): spec, React Flow board (`BreakdownBoard`, `PieceNode`), and a side panel that is also the keyboard path (`PiecePanel`: add by kind, list, name, required purpose; `ArrowPanel`: from, meaning, to). `boardModel.ts` holds the pure rules (new pieces, the grid layout with `columnsForBox` choosing columns from the board's measured shape through `useElementSize`, what blocks a submit, reference plans in the board's shape). Submit plan, then both boards side by side with the reference read-only, pattern chips, and the revision note (`RevisionPanel`); Grade my revision opens the loot. Positions are not saved (A355). The spec folds below 1300 wide; there the compare boards take the full width one above the other with the revise panel after them. The revise panel also lists the reference plan as text (`ReferencePlanList`). A boss opens on its breakdown.
- Build and refactor (`screens/quests/build/`): `BuildWorkspace` lays out three columns. Left `BriefColumn` (scaffold badge, brief, concepts, patterns after a pass, rubric, the "See it in the editor" checklist from `splitBrief.ts`), folded below 1600 wide and when the gaps open (A360); `BuildQuestView` opens it only from a fresh read of the save (A813). Center `EditorColumn` (file tabs, Monaco) with the worked-level `SelfExplanationGate` (`markedLines.ts`, keys `<file>:<line>`, A359). Right `RunColumn`: sticky Run, Submit, Open Editor; `RunProgress` (loop and reason, phases, elapsed, last line, Cancel); `LastSubmitCard` with Retry review; `TestList`; `BuildLog` (the result shown is the last Run, or the last Submit with its tests or compiler output, from the loot hand-off or the quest's latest stored attempt, so it survives a reload, A810, A814); `HintSpells` (the paid hints' texts come back with the quest as `revealedHints`; Reveal asks `revealHint` for the next index, so a repeat is answered free, A501, A502); `FailedSubmitOffers` (Parsons fallback; Seer Stone explained but disabled, A358). The column wraps its buttons and never scrolls sideways. Failures that are not the learner's code (M5.1h, A1213, A1214): a result with `unrelatedFailure` is labelled "could not build (not your code)" (never "did not compile") and shows `AppErrorView` with the headline "Not your code", the plain message, the suggested action, a Run again or Submit again button, and the details toggle; its build log starts closed. A Run or Submit that rejects shows its `AppError` through `AppErrorView` in compact form (headline "Not your code" for the environment codes in `outsideFailureCodes.ts`), and a rejected Submit (A1208) shows no loot, no last-submit card, no XP and no spree change, because the Build screen only opens loot when a Submit answers. Starting a Run clears a rejected Submit's error and the other way round (`QuestRun.clearError`), so an old message never sits beside a newer result. The fake marks a file with `// dojo:fake-outside-failure` to produce this failure (`FakeOutsideFailure.ts`); a Submit with it rejects and records nothing. Proof: `e2e/run-errors.spec.ts`.
- Loot (`LootScreen.tsx`, `screens/quests/loot/`): `ScoreCountUp` (the one reward motion of the score), `XpBreakdownList`, `ProgressChanges` (level and role, cards flipping in, skills, spree, streak, chest, unlocks, next reviews), then `ConceptResultsSection`, `BreakdownGradeSection`, or `CodeReviewSection` (provider and review score, fallback with Retry review (`retryReview` reviews the stored attempt again with no new attempt; `withRetriedReview.ts` swaps only the review into the loot entry, A740), rubric lines, findings by severity with a free Why? that can be asked again (A953), `ReflectionBox`: prefilled from the kept reflection, revisable, a self-check with its reason under Tests only, A951), the alternative after a pass (`AlternativePanel` from `getQuestAlternative`, with the reviewer's hint when an AI wrote one, A950), and Improve, See it run, Next. Next (the next open quest in the world, else back to the map, A812). Next takes focus once, when it first becomes known, and background refetches leave it alone (A815, A817). Next reviews name each day once, with past times as "due now" (A811, A816).

### Tests
- `tests/renderer/questHelpers.test.ts` (shuffle, board model, brief split, marked lines, routes, term matching) and `tests/renderer/fakeQuestFlow.test.ts` (the fake serves content's World 1, grading, runs, hints, the review timeout, concept, breakdown, drills).
- `e2e/ui-quests-flow.spec.ts` against `fake:quests`, each test in its own freshly launched app through the QA harness (`e2e/support/dojoTest.ts`), proofs copied only from passing tests: concept with predict, hover term, syntax check, questions, loot, Next; a drill through every item type with keyboard answers and Parsons distractors marked; a breakdown planned, submitted, compared, revised, graded; the compare step at 1100x700 with both boards at 0.6 zoom or more and the reference listed as text, and at 1440x900 opening with the reference and the revision note in view; a build through the gate, a failing Run, a hint, typed fixes, a passing Run, Submit with Retry review, Why?, reflection, See it run, Improve; two failed submits opening the Parsons fallback; every quest screen at 1100x700 and 1440x900 without sideways scroll in the stage or any side column (the breakdown board at least 450 pixels wide). Editor edits are made through the find widget once it reports exactly one match, and checked against the editor's lines in screen order. `SAVE_PROOF=1` writes `docs/iterations/m2-2-*.jpg`.
